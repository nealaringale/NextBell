Delete your NextBell account and cloud data

This page lets you request deletion even when you no longer have the NextBell app installed.

What will be deleted

After we verify that you control the NextBell Cloud Sync account, the developer will delete the associated Firebase Authentication account and the NextBell Firestore records stored under that account. This includes synced profile/settings, attendance, planner and expense/transaction records.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the address used for your NextBell Cloud Sync account.
  2. Use the subject NextBell account and data deletion request.
  3. Include the Firebase account email address. Do not send your password, bank SMS messages, or other financial credentials.
  4. We will verify the request and delete the Firebase account and associated NextBell cloud records.
Email a deletion request
Important: Signing out does not delete your cloud account. Cloud-account deletion does not automatically remove local-only records from a device or JSON backup files you exported. To remove local data, clear NextBell's app storage or uninstall the app; delete exported backup copies separately.
